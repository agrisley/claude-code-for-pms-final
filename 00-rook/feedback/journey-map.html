Action or job
A callout comes in. Rook ranks responders by routing priority.
A ping goes to the top-ranked responder's phone.
They take it, turn it down, or miss it within the ping wait.
If it isn't taken, it moves to the next responder.
Handlers reassure and file tickets. Support, Data and PM look for the cause.
