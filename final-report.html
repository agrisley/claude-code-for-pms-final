Executive summary

4.2 made a few responders go quiet, and our scoring rules keep them quiet.

Release 4.2 shortened the ping wait from 90s to 60s and re-weighted routing toward proximity. Pings that used to be answered started lapsing. The scoring code treats a miss like a decline, never forgives it, and only a taken ping earns points back. A responder who stops being offered pings has no way to recover.

What happened

Abrupt drop at release, not a slow slide. Data window is 29 Jun to 6 Sep, so "seasonal August dip" cannot be tested, and nothing in the sources supports it.

What we'd build

A miss stops lowering rank, a turn-down still does. Wait goes to 75s with a visible countdown. Handlers see who has gone quiet. Weights move in Stage 2 after 2 to 3 weeks of data.

How we stop a repeat

A reusable review-checklist skill runs four checks on any brief, and runs every Monday morning: owner, success measure, scope, problem before fix.

Not yet verified: why the four miss so often (phone delivery, the 60s wait, or availability). The brief has not been seen by Marcus Oyelaran or Wen Li.

Problem statement and what we set out to do

Callout tickets are about 4 to 12x normal since 4.2, and nobody could say why.

The problem

Since 4.2 shipped on 12 August, handlers write in about two things: "my phone never goes off" and "ping gone before I could answer". The hand-over said it was a seasonal August dip that would recover in September. No numbers supported that. The one-third theme fits the shorter wait; the two-thirds theme had no explanation.

Ticket volume: "3x normal" does not reproduce. It is about 12x for callout tickets only and about 4x for all tickets (support_tickets table).

What we set out to do

  • Find out what 4.2 actually did to responders, from data, not hunches.
  • Find the mechanism in the code and the people it happens to.
  • Design the fix from the point of view of the responder, as a clickable prototype, instead of quietly changing a number.
  • Make the review habit reusable so the next brief gets the same scrutiny.

Out of scope: reverting 4.2, fixing phone delivery, any identity inference (Security Policy 4.1), changes to Supply.

Start with "Send a ping" under the responder's phone, tap the notification on the lock screen, then watch the handler's view. Everything below is the live prototype, not a screenshot. Open it full size: prototype.html

1. A miss stops costing rank

A turn-down keeps its penalty.

2. 75s, with a countdown

Between today's 60s and the old 90s.

3. One-time rank restore

Everyone returns to a neutral value. Also wipes good history.

4. "Gone quiet" flag + snooze

Read-only for handlers. Snooze keeps you in the queue.

Optional, Helen's call: a dead-phone guard that skips a responder after about 3 misses in a row. Stage 2, after 2 to 3 weeks of data: recent acceptance weight 0.25 to 0.35, proximity held at 0.60.

How the skill keeps this from happening again

Each check maps to something that went wrong around 4.2.

OWNER

Every roadmap row was owned by Priya, who has left. Availability Confidence and Ping timeout tuning were committed for 4.2, never shipped, and nobody was asked.

Fact (Q3 roadmap). The check forces a named owner before a brief moves on.

MEASURE

"Ping timeout tuning" is one line. The 90s to 60s cut shipped without a miss-rate guardrail that I can find in the sources.

Inference: confirm with Helen. The check asks "how will we know it worked?" and the answer here would have been missed pings per responder.

SCOPE

The 14 Aug question went unanswered: was the proximity change meant for responders who turn pings down? The config does not distinguish them.

Fact (4.2 comments). The check compares what a brief says it covers at the start with what it commits to at the end.

PROBLEM FIRST

"Seasonal dip, it will recover" arrived before any evidence. It was repeated, with no numbers behind it.

Fact: nothing in the sources shows September data. The check puts the problem and its evidence ahead of the fix, with unknowns named.

A checklist is a floor, not a guarantee: it catches a missing owner or measure, not a wrong diagnosis. It does not replace talking to Wen Li.
