# Weekly update

Turn Annie's rough notes plus last week's update into this week's update. It gets pasted as plain text into a weekly Teams channel, so no tables, no headers, no markdown bold.

## What to ask for (only if not already given)

1. Last week's update (the baseline; carry forward anything unchanged).
2. This week's news, in any form: bullets, meeting notes, a brain dump. Per project: what happened, what's blocked, what's next.

If she gives only news, ask for last week's update. Don't ask more than that. If something is ambiguous (a date, a person, whether a step finished), ask rather than guess.

## Format: one block per project, in this exact order

```
<Area>: <Project name>
<One-sentence description of the study/goal>
Timing: <when>
Status: <On track | At risk | Off track>
Highlights: <what happened this week>
Blockers, Risk or Support Needed: <None. | specifics>
Next Steps: <what happens next>
```

Block order, matching past updates:
1. Annie's own projects, most time-sensitive first (surveys in field, then charters/approvals, then discovery work, then waiting/parked items).
2. A closing line: `<Lead name(s)> leading, Annie consulting/supporting:` followed by just the project titles, no detail. Leave it out if there are none.

Area prefixes seen so far: `Reference Lab`, `Rapid Assay`, `Vet Connect PLUS`, `Project Dora`. Use whatever prefix last week used for a given project.

## Writing rules (match her voice)

- Terse and factual. Fragments are fine ("Charter drafted and shared with Erica for review").
- Highlights are past tense and carry the concrete numbers and dates (n=244, sent 10/16, charter sent 9/30). Never round or invent a number.
- Next Steps are action-first and name the date or dependency when there is one ("Launch survey to DE 10/20 and AUS 11/16").
- Dates are m/d. Use first names for colleagues, as in past updates.
- Status is "On track" unless she says otherwise. Don't downgrade or upgrade it on your own.
- Blockers is `None.` unless she names one. If she mentions a delay, overlap or dependency that could slip a date, ask whether it belongs in Blockers or is only a Next Steps note. Past updates usually put it in Next Steps.
- Unchanged project: keep the block, Highlights `No progress this week.` plus the reason if known (e.g. "Waiting WalkMe to launch.").
- Finished since last week: say so in Highlights this week, then drop the block next week.
- New this week: write the description line from her words; leave Timing as `TBD` if she doesn't say.
- Project Dora has sometimes used a bulleted layout (`Status`, `Highlights/Key Achievements`, ...). Use the standard layout above unless she asks for the bullets.

## Output

Give the full update ready to paste, nothing before or after it except a short list of anything you assumed or left blank (e.g. "Timing for X set to TBD", "Didn't change Status on Y"). Do not save the update anywhere unless she asks.

## Check before sending back

- Every project from last week is present, or was intentionally dropped as finished.
- No fact appears that she didn't give you or that wasn't carried forward from last week's text.
- Dates that were in the future last week and are now past are either reported as done in Highlights or flagged as a question.
